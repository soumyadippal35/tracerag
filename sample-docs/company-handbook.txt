Company Handbook

Remote work is available three days per week for all full-time employees. Team leads approve exceptions when project delivery requires additional on-site time.

The learning budget is $1,500 per employee each calendar year. Employees submit receipts through the People Operations portal within 30 days.

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in the employee's local time zone. Teams may choose their remaining hours with manager approval.
